# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We want Paper Amigo to be a safe, respectful place to contribute. Participation
is open to people of every background, identity, experience level, and ability.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code without attacking the person behind them.
- Give specific, constructive feedback and accept it in good faith.
- Respect privacy, boundaries, and requests to stop unwanted contact.
- Make room for people who communicate differently or need more context.
- Take responsibility, apologize, and repair harm when you make a mistake.

Harassment, threats, sexualized attention, insults, discrimination, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in the repository, project discussions, project events, and
private communication connected to project work. It also applies when someone
publicly represents Paper Amigo.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not open a public issue about a conduct incident. Use GitHub's private report
option where it is available, or contact a maintainer privately through the
contact details on their GitHub profile. Include links, dates, and enough context
to review the report. Maintainers must protect the reporter's privacy as far as
the investigation allows.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a private warning, limit
participation, impose a temporary ban, or permanently remove someone from the
community. A maintainer named in a report must not take part in deciding its
outcome. Responses should match the severity, pattern, and effect of the conduct.
